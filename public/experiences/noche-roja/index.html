The Invitation

An evening composed
in agave & candlelight.

Beneath the red sky of Tequila, Hotel La Boutique opens its private courtyard for an evening composed in agave, candlelight, and ceremony. Noche Roja is an intimate celebration of craft, design, and Mexican luxury — created for guests who understand that true elegance is never loud.

The Setting

Patio de los Agaves

Patio de los Agaves is a secluded open-air courtyard framed by red stone, native agave, candlelit arches, and the quiet drama of Tequila's landscape. For one evening only, it becomes a private world in black, red, and gold.
