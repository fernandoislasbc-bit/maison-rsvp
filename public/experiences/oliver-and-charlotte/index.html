Chapter I

Once Upon
a September

"Before forever…
there was simply a hello."

It began the way all great stories begin — quietly, without announcement, in an ordinary moment that refused to stay ordinary. A glance. A pause. A beginning that neither of them fully understood until long after it had already become everything.

Chapter II

Growing
Together

"Every adventure quietly became home."

They discovered each other in the way seasons change — so slowly it is impossible to mark the moment of arrival. Coffee became ritual. Walks became journeys. Ordinary evenings became memories pressed like flowers between the pages of a life quietly becoming extraordinary.

What Oliver never expected was how easily she became the truest version of home he had ever known.

Chapter III

The
Question

"Beneath a golden sky,
among a thousand roses,
he knelt."

She said yes before he finished asking. And somewhere above them, the evening sky held perfectly still, as though the whole world had leaned in to listen.
